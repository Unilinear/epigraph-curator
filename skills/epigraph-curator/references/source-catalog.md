# Bundled Source Catalog

This is a small, offline-safe source layer, not a list of defaults. Every entry records original wording and a traceable witness. Select only when the line passes the editorial gates for the actual Target Document.

The quoted source texts below are public-domain originals to the best of the maintainers’ knowledge. Linked editions and websites may have their own terms. Do not copy a linked modern translation without checking its license.

## 1. Aristotle — desire to know

> Πάντες ἄνθρωποι τοῦ εἰδέναι ὀρέγονται φύσει.

- **Language / author:** Ancient Greek — Aristotle
- **Source:** *Metaphysics* Α.1, 980a21; [Perseus Greek text](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text.jsp?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0051%3Abook%3D1%3Asection%3D980a)
- **Semantic invariant:** Human beings possess a natural appetite for knowing.
- **Handles:** learning; curiosity; first principles
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 2. Marcus Aurelius — change and judgment

> ὁ κόσμος ἀλλοίωσις, ὁ βίος ὑπόληψις.

- **Language / author:** Ancient Greek — Marcus Aurelius
- **Source:** *Meditations* 4.3, closing maxim; [Perseus Greek text](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A2008.01.0641%3Abook%3D4%3Achapter%3D3%3Asection%3D1)
- **Semantic invariant:** The world is continual alteration, while lived experience is shaped by judgment.
- **Handles:** change; perception; framing
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 3. Vitruvius — firmness, use, beauty

> Haec autem ita fieri debent, ut habeatur ratio firmitatis, utilitatis, venustatis.

- **Language / author:** Latin — Vitruvius
- **Source:** *De architectura* 1.3.2; [LacusCurtius Latin text, University of Chicago](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/L/Roman/Texts/Vitruvius/1*.html#3.2)
- **Semantic invariant:** Architecture must answer simultaneously to durability, use, and beauty.
- **Handles:** design; fitness; craft; quality
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 4. Kālidāsa — word and meaning joined

> vāgarthāv iva saṃpṛktau vāgarthapratipattaye /<br>
> jagataḥ pitarau vande pārvatīparameśvarau //

- **Language / author:** Sanskrit — Kālidāsa
- **Source:** *Raghuvaṃśa* 1.1, identifier `Ragh_1.1`; [GRETIL e-text, Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_kAlidAsa-raghuvaMza.htm)
- **Semantic invariant:** Word and meaning belong together as inseparably as the divine pair invoked by the poet.
- **Handles:** writing; meaning; union; form
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 5. Zhuang Zhou — means and ends

> 荃者所以在魚，得魚而忘荃；蹄者所以在兔，得兔而忘蹄；言者所以在意，得意而忘言。

- **Language / author:** Classical Chinese — Zhuang Zhou, conventional attribution of the *Zhuangzi*
- **Source:** *Zhuangzi*, chapter 26, “External Things,” passage beginning `荃者所以在魚`; [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/what-comes-from-without/zh)
- **Semantic invariant:** Means matter because they reach an end, but should not be mistaken for that end once it is grasped.
- **Handles:** abstraction; language; tools; purpose
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 6. Liu Xie — discernment through breadth

> 凡操千曲而後曉聲，觀千劍而後識器。

- **Language / author:** Literary Chinese — Liu Xie (劉勰)
- **Source:** *The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons*, “Knowing the Tone,” paragraph 4; [Chinese Text Project, sentence `n108513`](https://ctext.org/wenxin-diaolong/zhi-yin/zh#n108513)
- **Semantic invariant:** Reliable taste is earned through wide, repeated encounter with actual works.
- **Handles:** criticism; craft; taste; pattern recognition
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 7. Matsuo Bashō — stillness made audible

> 古池や蛙飛こむ水のおと

- **Language / author:** Early modern Japanese — Matsuo Bashō
- **Source:** Bashō-attributed “Furuike” poem-slip and object commentary; [Idemitsu Museum of Arts](https://idemitsu-museum.or.jp/collection/calligraphy/basho/02.php)
- **Semantic invariant:** One small sound can disclose the depth of surrounding stillness.
- **Handles:** attention; simplicity; contrast; presence
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text. Early witnesses vary in orthography; this form follows the historically common *Kawazu-awase* reading.

## 8. Dante Alighieri — made for knowledge

> Considerate la vostra semenza:<br>
> fatti non foste a viver come bruti,<br>
> ma per seguir virtute e canoscenza.

- **Language / author:** Italian — Dante Alighieri
- **Source:** *Inferno* XXVI.118–120; [Princeton Dante Project](https://dante.princeton.edu/cgi-bin/dante/campuscgi/mpb/GetCantoSection.pl?INP_LEN=3&INP_POEM=Inf&INP_SECT=26&INP_START=118&LANG=2)
- **Semantic invariant:** Human dignity calls for pursuing virtue and knowledge rather than merely animal existence.
- **Handles:** learning; purpose; aspiration; ethics
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 9. Galileo Galilei — a legible universe

> La filosofia è scritta in questo grandissimo libro che continuamente ci sta aperto innanzi a gli occhi (io dico l'universo), ma non si può intendere se prima non s'impara a intender la lingua, e conoscer i caratteri, ne' quali è scritto. Egli è scritto in lingua matematica, e i caratteri son triangoli, cerchi, ed altre figure geometriche [...].

- **Language / author:** Italian — Galileo Galilei
- **Source:** *Il Saggiatore* (1623), section 6; [scan-backed transcription from *Le opere di Galileo Galilei*, vol. VI](https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Il_Saggiatore/6)
- **Semantic invariant:** Nature becomes intelligible through learning the representational language in which its regularities are expressed.
- **Handles:** science; notation; systems; legibility
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text. The bracketed ellipsis is explicit.

## 10. Johann Wolfgang von Goethe — mastery through constraint

> In der Beschränkung zeigt sich erst der Meister,<br>
> Und das Gesetz nur kann uns Freiheit geben.

- **Language / author:** German — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
- **Source:** “Natur und Kunst,” final two lines; [TextGrid Repository TEI text](https://textgridrep.de/browse/11dh9.0), handle `hdl:11858/00-1734-0000-0006-603A-1`
- **Semantic invariant:** Mastery appears in accepted limits, and enabling rules can create rather than extinguish freedom.
- **Handles:** constraints; craft; freedom; discipline
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 11. Michel de Montaigne — speech is co-owned

> La parole est moitié à celuy qui parle, moitié à celuy qui l’escoute.

- **Language / author:** Early modern French — Michel de Montaigne
- **Source:** *Essais* III.13, “De l’expérience”; [ARTFL Montaigne Project, 1588 text](https://artflsrv03.uchicago.edu/philologic4/montaigne1588/navigate/1/4/14)
- **Semantic invariant:** Meaning in speech is made jointly by speaker and listener.
- **Handles:** communication; interpretation; audience; meaning
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text; historical orthography preserved.

## 12. Henri Poincaré — facts require structure

> Le savant doit ordonner ; on fait la science avec des faits comme une maison avec des pierres ; mais une accumulation de faits n’est pas plus une science qu’un tas de pierres n’est une maison.

- **Language / author:** French — Henri Poincaré
- **Source:** *La Science et l’Hypothèse* (1902), part IV, chapter IX; [proofread scan transcription at Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/La_Science_et_l%E2%80%99Hypoth%C3%A8se/Chapitre_9)
- **Semantic invariant:** Information becomes knowledge through ordered relations, not accumulation alone.
- **Handles:** architecture; synthesis; information; science
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 13. Francisco de Goya — reason asleep

> El sueño de la razon produce monstruos.

- **Language / author:** Spanish — Francisco de Goya
- **Source:** Inscription on *Los Caprichos*, plate 43 (1799); [Museo Nacional del Prado object P002136](https://www.museodelprado.es/en/the-collection/art-work/the-sleep-of-reason-produces-monsters/e4845219-9365-4b36-8c89-3146dc34f280)
- **Semantic invariant:** When reason ceases to attend, imagination can generate destructive forms.
- **Handles:** attention; reason; imagination; failure
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text; the engraving’s unaccented `razon` is preserved.

## 14. John Keats — negative capability

> I mean Negative Capability, that is when man is capable of being in uncertainties, Mysteries, doubts, without any irritable reaching after fact & reason—

- **Language / author:** English — John Keats
- **Source:** Letter to George and Thomas Keats, 21–27 December 1817; [Keats Letters Project transcript and facsimile context](https://keatslettersproject.com/letters/negcapletter/)
- **Semantic invariant:** Creative intelligence can remain receptive inside uncertainty without forcing premature closure.
- **Handles:** uncertainty; attention; creativity; inquiry
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text. The surviving document is a contemporary transcript of a lost letter.

## 15. Emily Dickinson — truth by indirection

> Success in Circuit lies

- **Language / author:** English — Emily Dickinson
- **Source:** Poem Fr1263/J1129, Amherst Manuscript 372; [Amherst College manuscript record](https://www.digitalcommonwealth.org/search/commonwealth-oai:np193r41p)
- **Semantic invariant:** Truth may be communicated more successfully through calibrated indirection than blunt exposure.
- **Handles:** writing; rhetoric; revelation; audience
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.

## 16. Paul Klee — art makes visible

> Kunst gibt nicht das Sichtbare wieder, sondern macht sichtbar.

- **Language / author:** German — Paul Klee
- **Source:** “Schöpferische Konfession” (1920), section I, p. 28; [page-linked transcription and primary scan](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Sch%C3%B6pferische_Konfession:_Paul_Klee)
- **Semantic invariant:** Art does not merely copy appearances; it reveals what ordinary seeing leaves latent.
- **Handles:** design; perception; making; meaning
- **Rights note:** Public-domain source text.
